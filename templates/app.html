Support should be useful, not overwhelming.

In-app reminders are evaluated when Glim is open. Browser notifications require permission and are not delivered by a server while the app is closed.

Browser-local data

Glim stores your demo financial records in this browser using IndexedDB.

Your finance records are not sent to Glim's server for persistence and stay in this browser profile. Browser storage is not encrypted by Glim and is not a substitute for production account security. Download a private backup before resetting this profile.
