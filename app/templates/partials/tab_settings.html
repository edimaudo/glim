Practice controls

Pause temporarily stops Glim from recording practice progress, updating your streak or awarding points. It does not affect a bank payment. No money moves in this version.

Practice steps are active

While paused, Glim will not record a practice step or increase your streak or points.

Notifications

Choose quiet hours and how often Glim checks in. Quiet hours use your device’s local time.

Only in-app reminders are available in this version. Browser notifications are not configured.

Activity history

Review a plain-language history of changes made in Glim. These entries describe app actions, not bank transactions.

Privacy and data

Your plan is saved in this browser and is not synced across devices. You can export a copy or delete it here. Glim does not move money.

Planning only

Balances, recurring expenses and progress entries are illustrative. Recording a step does not pay a lender or transfer savings.

Your decisions stay yours

Glim offers estimates and learning prompts, not personalized professional financial advice.
