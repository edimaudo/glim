Help

Answers to common questions about your plan and its estimates.

How does Glim set the suggested amount?

It uses the monthly income and expenses you entered, your savings target, and the minimum balance you want to keep. Check the figures against your real budget before using the estimate.

What does Smart Strategy do?

It compares your active debt balances, interest rates, minimum payments, and modeled payoff results, then recommends a repayment order. You can switch to another strategy at any time.

What is the savings buffer?

It is cash you aim to keep available for unexpected costs. Glim shows your savings balance against the target, alongside the debt you still owe.

What does the payment visualization show?

It compares the estimated debt balance under your chosen strategy with a minimum-payment-only estimate. The figures are projections, not a guarantee of a lender's result.

Does Glim move money?

In this browser-based version, Glim helps you plan and calculate. It does not move money or make payments. Confirm balances, interest rates, and minimums with your statements.
