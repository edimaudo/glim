How does Smart Strategy work?

Smart Strategy compares debt repayment approaches using the balances, interest rates, minimum payments, income, expenses, and savings goal you have entered. Calculations use deterministic code; the recommendation is an estimate.

How are reminders chosen?

Choose a gentler reminder, a more direct interest estimate, or adaptive wording that responds to whether recent reminders were helpful.

How is payment streak measured?

Glim compares payments you record with the minimum payment and due day you entered. It does not verify payments with your lender, and you should update balances when interest or fees post.

When can I make a donation?

The donation section appears after at least one savings goal reaches its target. Donations are limited to the available savings amount.
