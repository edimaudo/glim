Practice controls

Pause temporarily stops Glim from recording practice progress, updating your streak or awarding points. It does not affect a bank payment. No money moves in this version.

Practice steps are active

While paused, Glim will not record a practice step or increase your streak or points.

Only in-app reminders are available in this version. Browser notifications are not configured.

Privacy and data

Your plan is saved in this browser and is not synced across devices. You can export a copy or delete it here. Glim does not move money.
